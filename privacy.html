Ledgerleaf privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-10-26

What Ledgerleaf stores

Ledgerleaf is a personal budgeting app for Android. Your wallets, transactions, categories and budgets are stored only on your phone. The developer runs no servers and never receives your data.

Google user data

If you turn on Google Drive backup, Ledgerleaf asks for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. It lets the app create, read and delete its own backup files in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive. Ledgerleaf cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

Ledgerleaf uses this access only to save backups you schedule or start, list them, restore one you choose, and delete backups older than the newest four. Backups go directly from your phone to your Google Drive. They are not sent to the developer or any third party, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. You can encrypt backups with a passphrase that never leaves your phone.

Ledgerleaf's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes the data on your phone. To delete the backups, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings, then Manage apps, find Ledgerleaf, choose Options, then Delete hidden app data. You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com under Security, Third-party apps and services.

Analytics and ads

Ledgerleaf contains no analytics, tracking or advertising.
